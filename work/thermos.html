3D product motion for Thermos — a cinematic animation of the iconic flask in a clean studio environment. Hyperrealistic material rendering (brushed steel, matte plastic, rubber) with studio lighting baked into CGI. A slow camera orbit around the product reveals its geometry and finishes. A product-visualization exercise designed for high-end digital advertising assets.
