Burberry Motion 3D

2025
Client
Burberry
Year
2025
Type
3D Motion · Fashion
Tools
Cinema 4D · After Effects

3D motion campaign for Burberry — a cinematic exploration of tartan fabric and the iconic codes of the British house, transposed into CGI. Simulated materials, precise reflections and a misty atmosphere form the visual through-line. The project follows a digital-luxury content logic, designed for high-end social platforms. Every frame oscillates between photographic realism and signature graphic abstraction.
