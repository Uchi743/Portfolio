Guerlain Motion 3D

2025
Client
Guerlain
Year
2025
Type
3D Motion · Luxury
Tools
Cinema 4D · After Effects

3D motion creation within Guerlain's universe — a luxury packaging animation that showcases the house's signature bottle through a refined visual choreography. The treatment of materials (blown glass, gold, amber liquid) relies on photorealistic shaders rendered in Octane. The pared-back set design draws on the visual codes of prestige perfumery: grazing light, dark background, shallow depth of field. A product-visualization exercise in service of brand emotion.
