Motion Design 3D

Apple Motion Exploration

2025
Client
Apple
Year
2025
Type
Motion Design · 3D
Tools
Cinema 4D · After Effects

Motion design exploration around Apple's visual universe — kinetic typography, fluid transitions and minimalist product aesthetics. The project plays on the tension between the brand's rigidity and the fluidity of 3D motion. Each sequence is conceived as a standalone micro-campaign, tailored for social and broadcast formats. A style exercise that pushes Apple's graphic codes into premium motion territory.
