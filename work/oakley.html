3D motion for Oakley — a sport product animation that stages the eyewear in a dynamic, energetic universe at the crossroads of sportswear and industrial design. The camera sweeps around the aerodynamic forms to reveal the construction and materials of the piece. Studio lighting baked directly into CGI accentuates the reflections on the polarized lenses. A render designed for digital advertising assets.
