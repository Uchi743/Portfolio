Prada Monolith 3D Modeling

2024
Client
Prada
Year
2024
Type
Fashion · Product Design · 3D
Tools
Cinema 4D · Octane Render

3D modeling of the Prada Monolith bag — a faithful reconstruction of the brushed-leather piece with its characteristic metal buckles, rendered in a clean studio environment in Octane. The work focuses on reproducing the micro-textures of the leather, the brushed reflections of the steel and the architectural volume of the bag. The sober, almost sculptural staging fits Prada's visual-communication codes. A project that illustrates the potential of CGI as a high-end product design tool.
